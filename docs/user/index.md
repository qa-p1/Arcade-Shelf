# Arcade Shelf

Arcade Shelf is a small floating place to collect files, folders, links and text while you work. Existing files stay where they are; Shelf keeps references and lets you gather selected items for your next task.

![Arcade Shelf expanded collection](../images/expanded.png)

## What Shelf is best at

- **Collecting without moving originals.** Drop files, folders, links or text on the capsule; file and folder entries remain references.
- **Keeping work in collections.** Switch between temporary and lasting collections, filter their contents, and pin the ones you want to keep.
- **Handing content to the next app.** Select items to drag or send to compatible Arcade actions, then keep returned outputs in Shelf.
